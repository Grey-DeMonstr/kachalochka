Kachalochka is a gym log. Record each set in a few taps and see how your weights grow.
Share your visits with friends and train together.

At the gym:
• Add a set with one tap: the app suggests the weight from your last visit
• See your previous results and your friends' results on the same machine
• Keep your machines with photos, tags and setup notes
• Record weight, distance, time or a simple count; assisted machines count weight down
• Set the platform weight, and whether a weight is total or for each side
• Use the rest timer, which restarts when you save a set
• Write comments for a machine or for the whole visit
• Prepare a plan of machines for your next visit

Progress:
• Charts of your best weight per machine for a month, 3 months, 6 months or a year
• A calendar of your visits
• Body measures: weight and girths, with the body fat they imply. Only you see them

Friends and family:
• Make a group and invite friends with a code or a link
• See the calendars, visits and machine photos of your group, in a colour for each friend
• Use several accounts on one phone: record your sets and your child's sets in one visit

The Android app works offline and without an account.
Sign in with Google to sync your data and to use the web app.

English and Russian. Dark and light theme.

Kachalochka - Changelog

1.6.4

- Machine search also finds a machine by the names of the friends' machines it is linked with.
- A machine shows the friends' machines it is linked with in the lists.
- Tapping a result in a machine's statistics opens that visit on the machine.
- The machine form has a statistics button.
- A machine's statistics show a trophy with the best set instead of a long title.
- Fixed: a child's photo is missing on its visit card in the calendar.


1.6.3

- The machine list has a search field and tag filters.
- Machine search finds names with a few typos.
- The link screen shows machine photos and every linked friend's machine.
- Copying a friend's settings also copies the machine's name and tags.
- Changing a machine's unit between kg and lb offers to convert its weights, also when merging.
- The add button of a set no longer shows the account name.
- Fixed: a child's sort order and tag grouping no longer reset after a sync.


1.6.2

- Linking to a friend's machine can copy its settings.
- Fixed: a friend's machine no longer shows its unit and step converted to yours.


1.6.1

- Linking a machine starts the search with the machine's name.
- A search field has a cross that clears it.


1.6.0

- Statistics can show only improvements, and a new machine is marked "New machine".
- Statistics can be exported as text.
- A visit can be saved as a plan from the calendar.
- The rest timer is only on a machine's page in a visit.
- The machine form is simpler: friends' tags are with your own tags.
- When a visit is grouped by tags, its machines do not repeat their tags.
- Fixed: on Android, the bar at the bottom of the screen is no longer light in the dark theme.
- Fixed: the avatar is no longer narrow in the top bar of a visit.


1.5.0

- Child accounts added: a parent can link a child's account and record the child's workouts.
- A friend's machine opens from their visit.
- When machines are grouped by tags, machines without a tag are under "Other".
- When two machines are merged, you choose which one stays, and the one used last is suggested.
- A changed platform weight can also change the weights of the sets already recorded.
- The privacy policy and terms describe child accounts.


1.4.3

- The app has its own icon.
- Starting a plan asks for confirmation first.
- A visit can be saved as a new plan.


1.4.2

- In the statistics, a new machine's first set is labelled "Start" instead of "Worst".


1.4.1

- Statistics can be sorted, including by best growth, and grouped by tags.
- Fixed: a statistics period now counts back from today instead of from the start of the month.


1.4.0

- Statistics added.
- Machine lists can be sorted by recent use, name or how often a machine is used.
- Machines already in the visit are listed last when adding one.
- Friends' photos of a machine are shown in its settings, and any photo can be its main one.
- Fixed: the first set on a machine now starts from the previous visit's best set.


1.3.2

- Fixed: on Android, the weight, reps and measure value disappeared, and - and + seemed to do
  nothing.


1.3.1

- Each machine of a visit opens on its own page, with its record, previous visit and friends.
- The home screen card is redesigned.
- Machines are shown as cards with tags, record and last use, and can be filtered by tags.
- Avatars: your Google picture or a photo of your choice, shown to your friends too.
- A friend's visit and the group screen are redesigned.
- The calendar shows your and your friends' visits the same way, each with a menu.
- A measure's value is entered on its screen, and its history is edited in a calendar.
- On the web, the footer shows only on the home and sign-in screens.
- Fixed: reps can now be typed, not only changed with - and +.


1.3.0

- Workout plans added.


1.2.0

- English language support added.
- Tag machines, and group a visit and its shared text by tag.
- Add a comment to any set.
- Gravitron is back as a way to count a machine's weight.
- Machines are called exercises in the Russian text.
- The set sheet closes completely.
- Machine photos show in the visit and the set sheet.
- In the visit, a machine's results sit under its name and its sets are numbered.
- Reordering the visit is now a button in the top bar.
- The calendar lists a visit's machines and their tags.
- All settings are applied with one button.
- Fixed: the next set no longer goes back to last visit's reps.
- Fixed: the previous visit in the set sheet is written the same way as everywhere else.


1.1.0

- Machine photos: take one with the camera or pick one from the gallery in the machine form,
  on Android and on the web.
- Machines in the list and the picker show their photo, or a linked friend's when yours has none.
- A friend's machine shows its photos.
- Delete your account and everything you recorded from the settings.
- The home screen shows the app version.
- Fixed: measures no longer appear twice in the measures list.
- Fixed: a friend's colour now shows the same everywhere, their own calendar included.
- Fixed: a friend's machine already linked to one of yours no longer offers to take it.


1.0.5

- Linked machines are listed by name and owner in the machine form, and each one opens.
- The set button shows progress until the set is saved.
- Shared visits mark the weights of a machine counted per side.
- Numbers are written with a decimal point; typing still accepts a comma.
- The manual body fat measure is removed along with its values.
- The unused left-and-right switch is gone from the machine form.


1.0.4

- Body fat is calculated under your measures by three formulas, and the measures they need are
  tagged.
- Sex, birth date and height are set in the profile section of the settings.
- Clearer measure names, and a hint on how to take each one in the measurement form.
- The manual body fat measure is kept only if you recorded values in it.
- Choose kg, lb or mixed weight units in the settings.
- Screen transitions are short, and their length is set in the settings; 0 turns them off.
- Machine results in a visit and friends' results read the same way as a shared visit.
- A machine's weight step is typed in its field.
- The web app links to its privacy policy and terms of service.
- Fixed: the next set no longer jumps back to last visit's weight after you save one.
- Fixed: a bright flash between screens in the dark theme.


1.0.3

- Body measures on the home screen: record them for any day and follow each one on a chart.
- A body fat calculator in the measures form, with three standard formulas.
- Friends' visits appear in your calendar as dots, in a colour you pick for each friend.
- Share a visit as text from the visit screen.
- A nickname in the settings, shown to your friends and in shared visits.
- The machine list shows your friends' machines too, and you can take one for yourself.
- Link your machine to a friend's, or merge two duplicates of yours, from the machine form.
- Machines and sets are reordered by dragging a handle.
- Fixed: your groups now appear right away on the friends screen.


1.0.2

- Groups of friends: create a group, invite friends with a link or a code, and see each other's
  visits and machines.
- Add a friend's machine to your visit to get your own copy linked to theirs; you then see each
  other's results on it, and either of you can unlink it.
- One visit per day: the home screen opens today's visit, with no start or end.
- Moving a visit onto a day that already has one offers to replace it.
- Change the order of machines and sets in a visit with the order button.
- A list of all your machines on the home screen, to add or edit them.
- A set's weight and a machine's weight step can be typed in, with decimals.
- Machines can use an own unit, such as plates, with its name shown next to every weight.
- The counterweight option is gone; such machines now count total weight.


1.0.1

- A calendar of your visits, opened from the home screen: add, open, move or remove a visit.
- The set panel on the visit screen closes with a swipe down or the back button.
- A new machine button at the end of the visit list adds the next machine.
- Fixed: on Android, machines, visits and sets now sync with the web version and other phones.
- Fixed: the web version no longer signs you out when you come back to it later.


1.0.0

- Initial version.

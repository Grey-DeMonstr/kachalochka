Kachalochka - Changelog

1.0.2

- Groups of friends: create a group, invite friends with a link or a code, and see each other's
  visits and machines.
- Add a friend's machine to your visit to get your own copy linked to theirs; you then see each
  other's results on it, and either of you can unlink it.
- One visit per day: the home screen opens today's visit, with no start or end.
- Moving a visit onto a day that already has one offers to replace it.
- Change the order of machines and sets in a visit with the order button.
- A list of all your machines on the home screen, to add or edit them.
- A set's weight and a machine's weight step can be typed in, with decimals.
- Machines can use an own unit, such as plates, with its name shown next to every weight.
- The counterweight option is gone; such machines now count total weight.


1.0.1

- A calendar of your visits, opened from the home screen: add, open, move or remove a visit.
- The set panel on the visit screen closes with a swipe down or the back button.
- A new machine button at the end of the visit list adds the next machine.
- Fixed: on Android, machines, visits and sets now sync with the web version and other phones.
- Fixed: the web version no longer signs you out when you come back to it later.


1.0.0

- Initial version.
